隐私政策

更新日期：2026-11-09 · 适用于 krinomatrix.com、KRINO WORLD 国内版（cn.krinomatrix.com）和海外版（app.krinomatrix.com）

我们是谁收集什么怎么用交给谁处理存在哪里、跨境保存多久你的权利未成年人安全变更与联系

01我们是谁

杭州决策矩阵科技有限公司（下称“我们”）运营本网站和 KRINO WORLD。KRINO WORLD 有两个相互独立的版本：国内版和海外版，各有各的服务器、数据库和账号，数据不互通。本政策说明我们处理哪些个人信息、为什么处理、交给谁处理，以及你能做什么。

02我们收集什么

  • 账号信息：用户名、显示名、密码（只保存加密后的哈希，我们看不到明文）、你在设置里的偏好。
  • 你和角色之间的内容：聊天记录；你创建、导入或修改的角色设定；角色从对话里记下的关于你的事（“记忆”）；工作台里生成和上传的角色形象。
  • 语音：只在你点麦克风或“语音对话”之后录音。录音用来识别文字或直接理解你的话，原始录音不保存；保存的是识别出来的文字和角色的回复。
  • 图片和摄像头画面：只在你主动发图或打开摄像头（需要先在设置里允许）后处理，用来理解画面。聊天里的原始图片和画面不保存，保存的是对话本身。
  • 使用记录：用了哪些功能、耗时多少、消耗了多少积分，用于计费、每日额度、排查故障和防滥用。
  • 技术信息：访问时的 IP 地址、浏览器类型等，出现在服务器日志里。

我们不收集你的通讯录、精确位置，也不做跨站追踪。

03我们怎么用

  • 提供服务：生成角色的回复、动作和声音；让角色记得你；生成角色形象。
  • 联网查询：你问到需要最新信息的问题（比如天气），或者让我们按已有作品写角色时，会把相关的提问交给搜索服务。
  • 计费与额度、安全与防滥用、排查故障。
  • 我们目前不会用你的聊天内容训练模型。如果将来要这样做，会先单独征得你的同意。

04交给谁处理

为了提供服务，我们会把完成该功能所需的内容交给下列服务方处理。我们不出售你的个人信息，也不用于广告。

服务方处理什么所在地
OpenRouter（转接 DeepSeek 等大语言模型）生成回复和角色设定时，发送对话上下文、角色设定和相关记忆；联网时发送搜索请求美国（模型服务方可能在其他国家）
阿里云百炼生成角色形象：发送形象描述和参考图中国
我们自己的 GPU 服务器语音合成、语音识别、直接听你说话、理解图片、设计角色声音中国
腾讯云网站和数据库所在的云服务器国内版：中国；海外版：日本东京

05存在哪里、跨境

国内版的账号和数据存在中国境内的服务器上；海外版的存在日本东京。但请注意：

  • 国内版：生成回复时，对话内容会经 OpenRouter 发往境外的模型服务。
  • 海外版：语音、图片理解和形象生成在中国境内处理。

使用本服务即表示你了解上述跨境传输。我们只传输完成该功能所需的内容，并要求服务方按其隐私条款保护数据。

06保存多久

  • 账号、角色、聊天和记忆：保存到你删除它们或注销账号为止。角色的记忆本身会随时间淡忘不重要的部分。
  • 注销账号：角色、记忆、聊天、你发布的作品会一并删除，不能恢复。
  • 计费和使用记录：保存到满足计费、对账和法律要求为止。
  • 服务器日志：只在排查故障和安全需要的时间内保存，之后按周期清除。

07你的权利

  • 查看和纠正：在聊天页的记忆面板里可以看到角色记得的每件事，记错了可以一键纠正或删除。
  • 导出：可以导出你的角色设定（导出不含你们的私人记忆）。
  • 撤回授权：随时在设置里关掉麦克风、摄像头、自动朗读、联网等功能。
  • 注销：在设置里注销账号，所有数据一并删除。
  • 其他请求（比如获取个人信息副本），请写信到 support@krinomatrix.com，我们会在 15 个工作日内答复。

08未成年人

KRINO WORLD 面向年满 18 周岁的用户。未满 18 周岁请不要注册使用。如果你发现未成年人在使用，请联系我们，我们会删除相关账号。

09安全

全站使用 HTTPS 加密传输；密码只存加密哈希；接口密钥只在服务器上；我们的 GPU 服务器在内网，只通过加密通道和网站服务器通信。没有绝对安全的系统；如果发生个人信息泄露，我们会按法律要求通知你和主管部门。

10变更与联系

政策有重要变化时，我们会在产品内或本页显著位置提前告知。有任何问题，请联系：杭州决策矩阵科技有限公司，support@krinomatrix.com。

Privacy Policy

01Who we are

Hangzhou Krino Matrix Tech Co., Ltd. ("we") runs this website and KRINO WORLD. KRINO WORLD has two separate editions, China and global. Each has its own servers, database and accounts, and data is not shared between them. This policy explains what personal information we process, why, who processes it for us, and what you can do.

02What we collect

  • Account: username, display name, password (stored only as a hash; we never see it), and your settings.
  • What you and your characters share: chat history; characters you create, import or edit; things characters remember about you ("memories"); portraits you generate or upload in the studio.
  • Voice: recorded only after you tap the mic or start a voice chat. It's used to transcribe or understand what you said. We don't keep the raw recording, only the transcribed text and the reply.
  • Images and camera: processed only when you send an image or turn on the camera (which you must first allow in settings), to understand what's shown. We don't keep the raw images or frames from chat, only the conversation itself.
  • Usage records: which features you used, how long they took and the credits they cost, for billing, daily limits, troubleshooting and abuse prevention.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type and the like, in server logs.

We don't collect your contacts or precise location, and we don't track you across sites.

03How we use it

  • To run the service: generate characters' replies, actions and voices; let characters remember you; generate portraits.
  • Web lookups: when a question needs fresh information (like the weather), or when you ask for a character from an existing work, the relevant query goes to a search service.
  • Billing and limits, security and abuse prevention, troubleshooting.
  • We do not currently use your chats to train models. If we ever want to, we'll ask for your separate consent first.

04Who processes it

To provide the service, we send the content a feature needs to the providers below. We don't sell your personal information or use it for ads.

05Where it's stored, and transfers

China-edition accounts and data are stored in mainland China; global-edition data is stored in Tokyo, Japan. Please note:

  • Global edition: voice, image understanding and portrait generation are processed in China.
  • China edition: when generating replies, conversation content is sent through OpenRouter to model providers outside China.

By using the service you acknowledge these transfers. We send only what a feature needs, and providers handle the data under their own privacy terms.

06Retention

  • Account, characters, chats and memories: kept until you delete them or close your account. Characters' memories also let unimportant details fade over time.
  • Closing your account deletes your characters, memories, chats and published works. This can't be undone.
  • Billing and usage records: kept as long as billing, reconciliation and legal requirements need.
  • Server logs: kept only as long as troubleshooting and security require, then cleared on a schedule.

07Your rights

  • See and correct: the memory panel on the chat page shows everything a character remembers, and you can correct or delete any of it in one click.
  • Export your character profiles (exports never include your private memories).
  • Withdraw consent: turn off the mic, camera, auto-read or web lookups in settings anytime.
  • Close your account in settings; all your data goes with it.
  • For anything else (such as a copy of your data), email support@krinomatrix.com. We reply within 15 working days.

08Minors

KRINO WORLD is for users aged 18 and over. If you're under 18, please don't sign up. If you learn that a minor is using it, contact us and we'll delete the account.

09Security

Everything travels over HTTPS; passwords are stored only as hashes; API keys stay on our servers; our GPU servers sit on a private network and talk to the web servers only through encrypted channels. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your personal information, we'll notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

10Changes & contact

We'll tell you in the product or prominently on this page before any significant change. Questions: Hangzhou Krino Matrix Tech Co., Ltd., support@krinomatrix.com.
