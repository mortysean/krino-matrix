Cookie 政策

更新日期：2026-11-09 · 适用于 krinomatrix.com 和 KRINO WORLD 两个版本

01一句话

我们不使用 Cookie 来追踪你，也不接任何第三方统计或广告。为了让网站正常工作，我们只在你自己的浏览器里（浏览器的“本地存储”）记几样必要的东西，它们不会自动随请求发给别的网站。

02具体记了什么

名称在哪做什么多久
km_lang公司官网记住你选的语言到你清除为止
km_cookie_ok公司官网记住你已经看过这条提示到你清除为止
krino_tokenKRINO WORLD保持登录（登录凭证）到你退出或凭证过期
krino_charKRINO WORLD记住你上次在和哪个角色聊到你清除为止
krino_autoreadKRINO WORLD记住“自动朗读”开没开到你清除为止
krino_devKRINO WORLD记住“开发者视图”开没开（设置 → 高级）到你关掉或清除为止

这些都是提供服务所必需的，所以不需要额外同意；它们不用于广告、画像或跨站追踪。

03第三方

本站不加载第三方统计、广告或社交插件，也不从第三方加载字体和脚本。

04怎么清除

在浏览器设置里清除本站的“网站数据”（或“Cookie 和网站数据”）即可。清除后需要重新登录，语言等设置会恢复默认。

05变更与联系

如果将来要使用新的 Cookie 或类似技术，我们会先更新本页，需要你同意的会先征得同意。问题请写信到 support@krinomatrix.com。另见隐私政策。

Cookie Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-09 · Covers krinomatrix.com and both editions of KRINO WORLD

01In short

We don't use cookies to track you, and we don't load any third-party analytics or ads. To make the sites work, we keep a few essential things in your own browser (its "local storage"). They aren't automatically sent to any other site.

02What we store

NameWherePurposeHow long
km_langCompany siteRemembers your languageUntil you clear it
km_cookie_okCompany siteRemembers you've seen this noticeUntil you clear it
krino_tokenKRINO WORLDKeeps you signed inUntil you sign out or it expires
krino_charKRINO WORLDRemembers which character you were talking toUntil you clear it
krino_autoreadKRINO WORLDRemembers whether auto-read is onUntil you clear it
krino_devKRINO WORLDRemembers whether developer view is on (Settings → Advanced)Until you turn it off or clear it

All of these are strictly necessary to provide the service, so they don't need separate consent. None are used for ads, profiling or cross-site tracking.

03Third parties

We don't load third-party analytics, ads or social widgets, and we don't load fonts or scripts from third parties.

04How to clear it

Clear this site's "site data" (or "cookies and site data") in your browser settings. You'll need to sign in again, and settings like language go back to their defaults.

05Changes & contact

If we ever use new cookies or similar technology, we'll update this page first and ask for your consent where it's needed. Questions: support@krinomatrix.com. See also the Privacy Policy.
