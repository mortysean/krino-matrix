// 03 · STACK

从决策到声音，自己造From decisions to voice, built in-house.

决策矩阵

Decision Matrix

每一刻判断该说话、该安静、该做个动作，还是该记下一件事——角色的“性格”落在行动上。

Every moment, a policy decides whether to speak, stay quiet, gesture or remember. Personality shows up as behaviour.

会遗忘的记忆

Memory that forgets

记忆按重要程度和时间衰减，关于你、关于自己、关于朋友的认知分开建模。

Memories decay by importance and time. What they know about you, themselves and their friends is modeled separately.

听、看、说

Hear, see, speak

直接听你的语气，看懂你发的画面，用自己的声音回答。

They hear your tone, understand what you show them and answer in their own voice.

自建推理

Own inference

语音合成、识别、听觉和视觉跑在我们自己的 GPU 上，低延迟、流式输出。

Speech, recognition, listening and vision run on our own GPUs, low-latency and streamed.

// 04 · PRINCIPLES

真实，但不越界Real, but within bounds.

R-01

AI 身份透明

Always labeled AI

每个角色都标明是人工智能。再像真人，也不会假装是真人。

Every character is labeled as AI. However real they feel, they never pretend to be human.

R-02

记忆属于你

Your memories are yours

角色记得什么你都能看到、能纠正；注销账号，角色、记忆和聊天一起删除。

You can see and correct everything they remember. Delete your account and the characters, memories and chats go with it.

R-03

只在你点了之后才听、才看

Mic and camera only when you ask

麦克风和摄像头只在你点按钮、浏览器授权之后打开，停下立刻关闭。

The mic and camera open only after you tap and your browser allows it, and close the moment you stop.

R-04

不碰的东西

What we don't do

不以现实中的真实人物为原型，不承载露骨内容，不做追踪广告。

No characters based on real people, no explicit content, no tracking ads.
