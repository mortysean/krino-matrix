Cookie Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-12 · Covers the international site (krinomatrix.com/global/) and KRINO WORLD's global edition

01In short

We don't use cookies to track you, and we don't load any third-party analytics or ads. To make the site work, it uses a single cookie to remember the region you picked. A few other essentials live in your browser's "local storage" and are never sent to any site.

02What we store

NameWherePurposeHow long

All of these are strictly necessary to provide the service, so they don't need separate consent. None are used for ads, profiling or cross-site tracking.

03Third parties

We don't load third-party analytics, ads or social widgets, and we don't load fonts or scripts from third parties.

04How to clear it

Clear this site's "site data" (or "cookies and site data") in your browser settings. You'll need to sign in again, and settings like your region go back to their defaults.

05Changes & contact

If we ever use new cookies or similar technology, we'll update this page first and ask for your consent where it's needed. Questions: support@krinomatrix.com. See also the Privacy Policy.
